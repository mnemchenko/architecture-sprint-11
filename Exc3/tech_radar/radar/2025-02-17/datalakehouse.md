A Data Lakehouse is an architectural approach that combines the flexibility of data lakes with the structured data management and reliability of data warehouses. It enables organizations to store and process both raw and structured data in a single system while supporting SQL queries, analytics, and governance requirements. This approach eliminates data silos, improves data consistency and accessibility, and enhances support for AI and machine learning workloads. Currently, we are trialing this methodology to evaluate its effectiveness in scaling analytics and improving data management practices.
