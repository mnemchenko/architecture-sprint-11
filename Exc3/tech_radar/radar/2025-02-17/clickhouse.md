ClickHouse is a high-performance, columnar database optimized for real-time analytical processing (OLAP). It is designed for fast query execution on large datasets, making it ideal for data warehousing, log analytics, and time-series data processing. ClickHouse supports distributed query execution, compression, and materialized views, allowing for efficient storage and retrieval of structured data. Currently, we are trialing ClickHouse to evaluate its performance, scalability, and suitability for analytical workloads within our data infrastructure.
