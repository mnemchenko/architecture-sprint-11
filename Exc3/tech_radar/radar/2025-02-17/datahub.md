DataHub is an open-source metadata management and data discovery platform designed to catalog, govern, and track data assets across an organization. It provides data lineage, impact analysis, and search capabilities, helping teams better understand and manage their data ecosystem. DataHub integrates with data lakes, warehouses, BI tools, and orchestration frameworks like Apache Airflow and dbt, making it a key component for data governance and observability. Currently, we are trialing DataHub to assess its metadata management capabilities, scalability, and integration potential within our data infrastructure.
