Apache Iceberg is a high-performance table format designed for managing large-scale analytical datasets in data lakes. It enables ACID transactions, schema evolution, hidden partitioning, and time-travel queries, improving reliability and query efficiency for structured data. Iceberg integrates with big data processing engines such as Spark, Trino, Flink, and Dremio, providing a scalable and flexible alternative to traditional data warehouses. Currently, we are trialing Iceberg to assess its suitability for structured data management, metadata handling, and analytics workloads in our data infrastructure.
