PowerBuilder is a legacy application development framework primarily used for client-server applications. While it was once popular for enterprise desktop applications, its adoption has significantly declined due to limited modernization, lack of cloud support, and a shrinking developer community.

Although newer versions (such as those from Appeon) have introduced some improvements, PowerBuilder is no longer a recommended choice for new projects due to its outdated architecture, limited integration with modern technologies, and declining ecosystem support.

We are going to hold it for a while to provide space for new data technologies transformation. After that, It will be replaced with modern technologies.
