Nessie is a Git-like version control system for data lakes, enabling branching, tagging, and time-travel queries for structured datasets. It provides a transactional catalog for managing tables in formats like Apache Iceberg, Delta Lake, and Hive, ensuring consistency and reproducibility across multiple data processing engines. By decoupling metadata from compute, Nessie improves data governance, collaboration, and rollback capabilities. Currently, we are trialing Nessie to evaluate its impact on data versioning, reproducibility, and metadata management within our data infrastructure.
