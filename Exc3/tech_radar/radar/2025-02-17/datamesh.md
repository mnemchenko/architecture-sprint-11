Data Mesh is a decentralized data architecture approach that treats data as a product, enabling domain-oriented ownership and self-serve data infrastructure. It shifts away from centralized data teams and monolithic data warehouses, promoting scalability, flexibility, and improved data governance across distributed teams. By enforcing federated governance and interoperability, Data Mesh enhances data accessibility and quality while reducing bottlenecks in data processing. Currently, we are trialing this approach to assess its impact on data collaboration, autonomy, and scalability within our organization.
