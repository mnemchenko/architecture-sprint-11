Dremio is a high-performance SQL query engine designed for fast analytics on data lakes without requiring data movement or duplication. It enables self-service data exploration, accelerates queries with columnar caching and query reflection, and supports open table formats like Iceberg, Delta Lake, and Hudi. With its semantic layer and native integrations with BI tools, Dremio simplifies data access for analysts and engineers. Currently, we are trialing Dremio to evaluate its performance, scalability, and suitability for interactive analytics within our data infrastructure.
