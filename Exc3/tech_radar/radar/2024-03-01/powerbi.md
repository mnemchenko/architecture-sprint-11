Power BI is a business intelligence (BI) tool for data visualization, reporting, and analytics. It enables users to create interactive dashboards, generate insights, and explore data from multiple sources with ease. With built-in AI capabilities, seamless integration with SQL databases, cloud services, and spreadsheets, and support for self-service analytics, Power BI empowers teams to make data-driven decisions efficiently. Its adoption enhances reporting capabilities, collaboration, and overall business insights across the organization.
