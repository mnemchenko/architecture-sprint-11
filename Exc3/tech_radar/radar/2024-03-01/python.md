Python is used for AI services development.

Python is a versatile and widely adopted programming language known for its simplicity, readability, and extensive ecosystem. It is particularly popular for AI and machine learning due to its rich set of frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and scikit-learn. Python also excels in backend development, automation, and data science, making it a key language for building scalable AI services and data-driven applications. Its strong community support and vast libraries ensure continued relevance and innovation.
